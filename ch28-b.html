2
The Story
Read carefully. Notice the bold words.
🌾

🌸 Long ago, the Romans told a story about Ceres, the goddess of the harvest. Ceres made the crops grow. Farmers prayed to her every spring, because without Ceres there would be no wheat, no fruit, and no bread.

🌳 One sunny day, Ceres and her daughter Proserpine were roaming through the woods. Something magical happened wherever Ceres walked. When her feet touched the earth, ripe grain sprang up. When her hands touched a tree, fruit blossomed on its branches. Proserpine followed behind her mother. She was as beautiful as springtime, with long golden hair.

💧 After a while, Ceres felt thirsty. She stopped to drink from a cool stream. While her mother was drinking, Proserpine wandered away. Nearby, she saw a clump of white lilies. 'How lovely!' she said, and she bent down to pick them.

🕳️ Suddenly, the ground opened beneath her feet! Proserpine fell into the darkness and disappeared. When Ceres looked up from the stream, her daughter was gone. 'Proserpine!' she called. 'Proserpine! Where are you?' But there was no answer. Only the wind moved through the trees.

🔍 Ceres was terribly worried. For fourteen days, she wandered over the whole earth, looking for her lost daughter. She searched the mountains, the valleys, and the seashore. She did not stop to eat or rest.

🧚 At last, Ceres met a nymph, a spirit who lived in the rivers. The nymph came close and whispered, 'I have just come from the Underworld, the land of the dead. I saw your daughter there! Pluto, the king of the Underworld, has stolen her. He fell in love with her and wants her to be his wife. He opened the ground so that she could walk down into his kingdom.'

Ceres had always helped the earth — now, in her sadness, she turned against it.

🍂 When Ceres heard this, she was furious. 'I have helped the ground to grow crops!' she shouted. 'And this is how it thanks me! It has swallowed my daughter! I will curse it until it is dry and empty of all life!' At once, the trees around her turned brown. The leaves fell. The grass died, and the flowers dried up.

☁️ Still full of rage, Ceres climbed up into the heavens. She went all the way to the palace of Jupiter, the king of the gods. 'Jupiter!' she cried. 'Make Pluto give my daughter back to me! If you do not, I will never let spring come to the earth again. There will be no fruit, no grain, and no grass. The earth will be as dead and hard as my heart!'

🤔 Jupiter was silent. He knew that the people on earth needed food. But he also knew that Pluto would not want to give up Proserpine. What would the king of the gods decide? You will find out in the next lesson.

Language Focus
Grammar from the story — learn through meaning
📌 Past continuous + past simple
Use was/were + -ing for a longer action, and past simple for a short action that interrupts it.
While Ceres was drinking, Proserpine wandered away.
They were roaming through the woods when it happened.
Proserpine was picking lilies when the ground opened.
Ceres was looking for her daughter when she met a nymph.
💡 Tip: 'While' usually goes with the longer action.
📌 Wherever / whenever
Use wherever (any place) and whenever (any time) to show something always happens.
Wherever Ceres stepped, ripe grain sprang up.
Whenever she touched a tree, fruit blossomed.
Wherever she looked, there was no answer.
Whenever farmers needed rain, they prayed.
💡 Tip: Wherever = in every place that; whenever = every time that.
📌 First conditional: If you don't..., I will...
Use If + present simple, will + verb to talk about a real result in the future.
If you don't help me, I will never let spring come.
If Pluto keeps her, the earth will die.
If Jupiter agrees, Ceres will be happy.
If you look carefully, you will find her.
💡 Tip: Don't use 'will' after 'if' — say 'If you help', not 'If you will help'.

Ideas & Society
Think about big ideas. There is no wrong answer.
👩‍👧
A mother's love. Ceres searched for fourteen days without rest. What does this story tell us about parents and children?
😡
Anger and others. Ceres was angry with Pluto, but the farmers and animals suffered. When one person is angry, who else can be hurt?
🌍
People and nature. In the story, the earth dies when the goddess is sad. How do people today affect the health of nature?
⚖️
Asking for justice. Ceres went to the king of the gods for help. Where do people go today when they want something unfair to be fixed?
