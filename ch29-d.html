The Story
Read carefully. Notice the bold words.
🏔️

⚔️ The Romans and the Carthaginians fought back and forth for a long time. Sometimes Rome won a battle. Sometimes Carthage won. But neither side could win the whole war. Then one of the Carthaginian generals had a wonderful, surprising idea. Instead of attacking the Romans with ships, he would attack them with elephants!

🏔️ Far in the north of Italy stand the Alps. They are tall, cold mountains, covered with snow and ice. One winter, a group of Roman soldiers was camping near these mountains. They sat by their fires, wrapped in their cloaks. They felt safe. Who could ever come over those terrible mountains?

👂 Then, late one evening, they heard strange noises. There was a deep rumbling, like thunder far away. There were loud, high trumpet calls that no Roman had ever heard before. The soldiers peered out of their tents into the swirling snow and mist.

🌫️ Suddenly, huge dark shapes loomed up in the snow. The ground began to shake. A herd of elephants was charging straight through the camp!

Out of the swirling snow came huge dark shapes — and the ground began to shake.

😱 The Roman soldiers fled in terror. Many of them had never seen an elephant before. They did not even know what these giant grey animals were! Some thought they were monsters.

🎨 And these were no ordinary elephants. They were specially trained for battle. When they were told to attack, they spread their ears wide to make their heads look even larger. Their heads and ears were painted red, white, and yellow, to make them look even more terrifying.

🏹 Some of the elephants were pulling carts full of armed Carthaginian soldiers, who shot arrows at the Roman troops. Others carried wooden boxes on their backs. Even more soldiers were hiding inside these boxes, ready to attack.

🐎 Some brave Romans tried to fight back. They ran for their horses and mounted, ready to ride straight at the thundering beasts. But the horses had never smelled or seen elephants either. They were stricken with terror. They screamed, turned around, and bolted, carrying the Roman soldiers away into the dark.

❄️ By morning, the camp was empty and broken. Footprints as big as tables covered the snow.

❓ How did elephants get to the top of Italy? Elephants live in hot lands, not in snowy mountains! Who planned this amazing attack? In the next lesson, you will meet the clever general from Carthage. His name was Hannibal.

Language Focus
Grammar from the story — learn through meaning
📌 Past continuous + past simple: 'were camping when…'
Use past continuous for a longer action, and past simple for a sudden action that interrupts it.
The soldiers were camping when they heard strange noises.
They were sitting by the fire when the ground shook.
The elephants were charging through the camp.
The snow was swirling when the shapes appeared.
💡 Tip: Long action = was/were + -ing; sudden action = past simple.
📌 Making it stronger: 'even + comparative'
Use 'even' before a comparative to say 'more than before'.
They spread their ears to make their heads even larger.
The paint made them look even more terrifying.
The boxes held even more soldiers.
The night grew even colder.
💡 Tip: 'Even' adds surprise: it was already big, now it is bigger.
📌 Irregular past: flee → fled, shake → shook
Some common action verbs have irregular past forms. Learn them in groups.
The soldiers fled in terror. (flee)
The ground shook. (shake)
They ran for their horses. (run)
The elephants spread their ears. (spread — no change)
💡 Tip: Say the three forms aloud: flee–fled–fled, shake–shook–shaken.

Ideas & Society
Think about big ideas. There is no wrong answer.
🐘
Animals in war. Elephants and horses were used in ancient wars. How do you think the animals felt? Should animals ever be used in human fights?
😨
The power of surprise. The Romans were beaten by something they had never seen. Why is the unknown so frightening to people?
🏔️
Feeling safe. The Romans felt safe because of the mountains. Is it dangerous to feel too safe? Can you think of an example?
💡
Thinking differently. The general used elephants instead of ships. When is it smart to solve a problem in a completely new way?
