The Story
Read carefully. Notice the bold words.
🧭

😟 Hannibal was still in Italy. Year after year, his army marched up and down the country. The Romans tried to stop him, but Hannibal kept winning. How could Rome defeat such a clever enemy?

💡 Then a young Roman general named Scipio had an idea. He thought, "Why should we keep fighting Hannibal here, in our own land? Hannibal has brought the best soldiers of Carthage to Italy. That means Carthage itself is almost empty of soldiers. Let's go and attack Carthage!"

⚔️ Scipio gathered together the best Roman soldiers he could find. He put them on ships and sailed south across the sea to North Africa. He did not go to fight Hannibal. Instead, he went straight to Hannibal's home city.

Scipio did not chase Hannibal. He made Hannibal come to him.

🏙️ The city of Carthage was not expecting to be attacked! For years, the war had been far away, in Italy. Now, suddenly, Roman soldiers were outside the city walls. The best Carthaginian fighters were with Hannibal, hundreds of miles away. Carthage was almost unprotected.

📨 The leaders of Carthage were frightened. They quickly sent a message across the sea to Hannibal. It said: "Come back to Carthage and help us!"

🤔 Imagine how Hannibal felt when he read that message. He had spent many years fighting in Italy. He had come so close to Rome. But now his own homeland was in danger. He had no choice. He had to leave Italy and sail home to defend his city.

⛵ Hannibal and his army got on their ships and sailed across the sea back to Africa. But his soldiers were worn out. They had spent years marching, fighting, burning, and sacking towns in Italy. They were tired, hungry, and far from strong.

🛡️ Scipio's army was waiting for them. The two great generals finally met in battle on African land. This time, Hannibal's tired soldiers could not win. The Romans defeated them! Scipio became a hero in Rome, and the Romans later gave him a new name: Scipio Africanus, because he had won in Africa.

🏃 And Hannibal? The great general who had crossed the Alps with elephants now had to flee. He ran away and hid far to the east, in a land called Asia Minor. His dream of beating Rome was over.

🏛️ Scipio's plan teaches us something important. Sometimes the best way to solve a problem is not to fight it head on, but to look at it from a different side.

Language Focus
Grammar from the story — learn through meaning
📌 Past perfect: had + past participle
Use the past perfect for an action that happened before another past action.
Hannibal had spent many years in Italy before he went home.
The war had been far away for years.
He had come so close to Rome.
💡 Tip: Past perfect = 'the past before the past'.
📌 'Instead' and 'instead of'
Use 'instead' to show that someone chose a different action.
Scipio did not fight Hannibal. Instead, he went to Carthage.
He attacked the city instead of chasing Hannibal.
Instead of staying in Italy, Hannibal sailed home.
💡 Tip: After 'instead of', use a noun or verb + -ing.
📌 Had to (past obligation)
Use 'had to' + verb to say something was necessary in the past.
Hannibal had to leave Italy.
He had to defend his city.
The great general had to flee.
💡 Tip: 'Had to' is the past form of 'have to' and 'must'.

Ideas & Society
Think about big ideas. There is no wrong answer.
🧭
A different angle. Scipio solved his problem by attacking somewhere else. Can you think of a problem in school or life you solved in an unusual way?
🏠
Home comes first. Hannibal left Italy to save his homeland. Why do people feel so strongly about their home country?
😴
Too tired to win. Hannibal's soldiers lost because they were worn out. Why is rest important, even for strong people?
🏅
Becoming a hero. Scipio became a hero in Rome, but to Carthage he was an enemy. Does a hero depend on whose side you are on?
