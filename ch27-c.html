The Story
Read carefully. Notice the bold words.
🐺

Let us go back to the Tiber River. The basket with the two baby boys was stuck in the root of the fig tree. The twins were crying loudly. And a wolf heard them!

👀 The wolf was a mother, and she had cubs of her own. She came closer very slowly and peered around the trunk of the fig tree. She saw the basket, and inside were two hungry babies. Most wolves would run away from people. But this wolf felt pity for the little boys.

🏃 She tugged the basket up onto the bank. Then she carried it, bit by bit, back to her den. Inside the den, she fed the twins together with her own wolf cubs. For many days, the human babies drank the wolf's milk and slept in the warm den. She raised them as if they were her own children.

The wolf raised the babies with her own cubs, as if they were her own.

🐑 One day, a shepherd was walking in the hills. He was looking for a lost lamb. Suddenly he heard a happy coo and a funny gurgle. The sounds came from the brush near the wolf's den. The shepherd pushed some branches aside. What do you think he saw?

😲 He saw two fat, happy baby boys, playing naked among the wolf cubs! The shepherd could not believe his eyes. He and his wife had no children of their own. He knew at once what to do. He picked up the boys and took them home.

🏡 The shepherd and his wife loved the twins like their own sons. They gave them food, clothes, and a warm bed. They taught them to look after sheep and to work hard. The boys grew up strong and clever. When they were grown, they were tall, handsome young men.

🐕 Think about the people who saved Romulus and Remus: a servant, a wolf, and a shepherd. None of them was rich or powerful, but each one helped at just the right time. The wolf became a famous symbol of Rome. Even today, you can find pictures of a wolf with two babies on statues and coins in Italy.

🌿 Life with the shepherd taught the twins how to be brave. They climbed hills, crossed streams, and learned how to protect the flock from wild animals. These skills would be useful when they built a town of their own. Listen to the next part of the legend to find out where they went when they grew up.

Language Focus
Grammar from the story — learn through meaning
📌 Past simple and the verb 'saw/heard'
Use 'saw' and 'heard' for senses in the past.
The wolf heard them crying.
She saw the basket with the babies in it.
He heard a coo and then a gurgle.
💡 Tip: See-saw, hear-heard, find-found.
📌 Prepositions of movement
Use these words to show where someone goes.
She tugged the basket up onto the bank.
She carried it back to her den.
He pushed the branches aside.
💡 Tip: Movement words: up, onto, back to, aside, through.
📌 'As' for comparison: 'as her own'
Use 'as' to say someone is treated like something else.
She raised them as her own.
He loved them as his own sons.
She fed them as she fed her cubs.
💡 Tip: as + noun = like / in the role of.

Ideas & Society
Think about big ideas. There is no wrong answer.
🐺
Kindness from unexpected places. A wolf saved the babies. Have you ever received help from someone you did not expect?
👨‍👩‍👦
What makes a family? The shepherd and his wife raised children who were not born to them. What do you think makes a family a family?
🏙️
National symbols. Rome chose a wolf as its symbol. What animal represents your country and why?
🗣️
Legends and truth. This is a legend and may not be true. Why do people keep telling stories that may not be real?
